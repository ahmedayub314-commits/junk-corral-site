FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the things people ask us most.

How much does junk removal cost?

You pay for the space your items take up in the trailer, not by the hour. Prices start at our minimum for a single item and go up to a full trailer. You get a flat number before we start, and it does not change once we are loading.

Do you offer same-day service?

Usually, yes. Most of our jobs are same day, and if you catch us early we can often be there within a couple of hours. There is no rush fee for it.

What items do you remove?

Furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, garage and attic clutter, yard waste, construction and renovation debris, and whole-house cleanouts. If it fits in the trailer and it is not hazardous, it goes.

Do I need to move everything outside?

No, and you never will. Carrying it out is included in the price, whether it is in the basement, the attic or on the third floor. You point at it, we do the rest.

What happens to my junk after you take it?

Anything still usable goes to a donation centre and metal goes to recycling. Only what is genuinely finished goes to the transfer station.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Irving and work across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. If you are a little outside our usual range, call anyway and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth the drive.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card, cash, Zelle, Cash App and bank transfer, and we invoice for commercial and property management accounts. You pay when the trailer is loaded, never before.
